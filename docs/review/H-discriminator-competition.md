# Appendix H — The discriminator design competition

Five designs (mandates: explicit predicates, sequential evidence, minimal delta, track-and-group, free hand) were built against
the `rtasim` corpus, each independently audited (numbers re-run, code read against its own documentation, new hostile scenarios
written to break it), then judged by two independent judges who re-measured what they needed. Branch names refer to the review
machine's worktrees and are kept as provenance; the winning design plus the judges' fixes and grafts is what ships on
`review/detector` (see `docs/DETECTOR.md`).

Every design false-cuts the same three physical classes — a family-less line swelling dB-linearly (flute/soprano messa di voce, sine pad, theremin, organ swell pedal, a channel-fader ride on a held organ note), a family-less line already sounding at arm above an absolute level gate (organ note/chord, projector whine at −36/−40), and a loud crowd whistle at −7…−2 dBFS — and every design except sequential-evidence never reports the loop brief's *normal* watch-mode howl (≥300 dB/s into a limiter/compressor plateau below −10 dBFS: B2, Y3, A1-fast, A2, A9, B6: 0/3 each for predicates, free-hand, minimal-delta; track-and-group 1-3/18). Those two facts frame everything below: the corpus separated the designs on *programme statistics* (hold-out seeds, analyser sweeps), the breakers did not separate them on FP class, only on how conservative each is.

## 1. Ranking

**1. disc-predicates (wt/disc-predicates @ 2fc52ee) — winner.** It is the only design whose programme-FP surface is a strict subset of every other's (18 breaker scenarios ⊂ free-hand's 21; 0 FP on seeds 1-3 in all 8 mode/loop runs, 1 FP on each hold-out triple, 0 FP on attack_k 1.0 / BQ / skirt 2 / decay 4 s / RMS / noise ×1.5, and — verified here — 0 FP with the LF window opened to 40 Hz on S1/S7/X5/X15/S17/B9/B10 under default, attack_k 1.0 and BQ analysers, i.e. the M7 40/80 Hz mechanism stays dead *inside* the window, which track-and-group could not do). Its decision model is the brief's Q4 written as code: BASE (narrow ∧ no co-moving family ∧ stationary ±0.25 band ∧ not decaying ∧ new-or-at-arm ∧ in window, `_judge` detector.py:1037-1110) is published as a candidate and never cut in watch; a cut needs one piece of positive evidence (RISE ≥ 6 dB of the line's *own* level after the band could have settled, LOUD, AT-ARM, PROBE), none of which is growth-rate. The analyser settle rule (⌈1.5·k/(Δf·T) − 0.5⌉ frames, `analyser_rise_k` 1.0 = the critically-resolved bound, not the simulator's 0.5/Δf) is the actual M7 fix and is model-free; the common-mode reference as the median of per-band *changes* over signal bands (`_cm` :626) is the only CM implementation on the panel that survived the RMS sweep; the probe has the right shape (two over-responses, STATIONARY class for 1 dB/dB lines, pre-emptive emission, `_probe` :996). Every threshold in its DESIGN.md carries a physical origin, the corpus evidence that moved it, and a list of where the lead's numbers were wrong — that is what Jim needs to calibrate on the desk. Its costs are real and all on the *miss* side: it relaxed `test_ring_detected_quickly` from ≤6 to ≤9 frames (400 ms for a 15 dB/s ring), misses X11 and every fast-howl-to-moderate-plateau breaker (MODERATE forever), cannot see a rise slower than ~2.3 dB/s (`run_lv` deque maxlen 64, :470 — Y7's 39 dB climb missed), cannot emit a 14-17 dB plateau present at arm (Y8/A8), and its absolute `loud_line_db −10` / `arm_line_min_level_db −40` float on `/-prefs/rta/gain` (gain +12 → 53 FP; the mandated sweep was skipped). Two plain bugs: the undocumented `_arm_lines` re-association (:813-817, Y2 15 FP) and `del self._steps[0]` invalidating `judged_steps` indices after 32 master steps (:592-593, probe silently dies late in a long ring-out). All of these are additive fixes on a sound skeleton; none requires removing a load-bearing wall.

**2. disc-free-hand (wt/disc-free-hand @ 6b865a4).** Best corpus numbers on the panel that also survive scrutiny: 117/117, 0 FP, hold-out 114/117 with 0 FP, 12/14 analyser variants 0 FP, median latency 100 ms, 100/117 ≤ 300 ms, X11 caught at ≤ 49 ms, and (verified here) the same LF robustness as predicates with *no hard floor at all* (settle = 3/Δf, `_growth` :849). Its growth machinery (GROW-fast: ≥3 consecutive steps ≥2 dB summing ≥12 dB with shape limits; GROW-slow over settle-bounded windows; back-fill of newborn tracks from the spectrum ring buffer `_backfill` :1264; co-moving-family veto for growing lines `_family_comoving` :750; independent-partner release `_independent` :722; track resurrection through a crash) is the best on the panel and is what predicates lacks. It ranks second, not first, because its FP surface is strictly larger in the classes that matter during a show — GROW-slow tapers to 4 dB at ≥2 s and 1.5 dB/s (Leslie AM half-cycle Y11: 9 FP; organ swell chord A6: 40; fader ride Y9), EST fires 250 ms after arm with no dwell (organ chord at arm A10: 45 FP vs predicates 10), the sticky `emit_level` ratchet (:1252, :1436) drives every wrong cut to −9 dB in 2.3 s in direct contradiction of loop brief §4.1, and `lf_strict_hz` (:1410, :1474-1489) disables CLIP/LOUD/EST below 160 Hz so a full-scale 100 Hz howl (Y14) or an established 122 Hz body howl (Y4) is unreportable in any mode. The `pop ⇒ never cut passively below −10 dBFS` rule is the M7 false negative moved from "plateaued" to "fast" (Y3 plateau sweep: −9 caught, −10.5…−30 never) — predicates has the same blind spot, but as missing evidence rather than as a veto, which is why predicates is the easier base. It also rewrote `test_held_note_not_detected`'s stimulus and added a test asserting a family-less 80 dB/s ramp IS feedback — physically defensible (L §1.3-1.4) but it enshrines the swell-FP class as a requirement.

**3. disc-track-and-group (wt/disc-track-and-group @ 480eed8).** The grouping idea — a partial counts only if its *track* is compatible (co-born without divergence, or envelope/increment-correlated), single partners need exact position + co-birth/co-growth, "pending" holds instead of vetoes (`_group` L712, `_compatible` L652) — is the most physically complete harmonic test on the panel and the informed run is 117/117 with 0 FP. But it fails priority 1 where the two above do not: 6 FP on hold-out seeds 4-6 of ordinary corpus programme (flute, whistle, organ, choir, all `tier=B onset=slow`), 29 FP at RTA decay 4 s, 33 at release-law 17, 13 + 4 HARM under RMS; and the moment a kick mic is declared (`lf_feedback_possible=True`) the M7 40 Hz cut reappears (S19 16 FP, B10 21 FP, S7 4 FP — its claim that "LF programme inside the window is rejected by physics" was never tested inside the window). Its one-shot 3-frame onset classifier (`_classify_onset` L961) is load-bearing in both directions: `adult` is a permanent watch-mode veto (B2 fast wedge ring 25-29 dB prominent, never cut) while `ramp/slow` admits any rise spread over ≥3 frames (solo sine-pad swell B1 24 FP). Integration hazard: the new invariant `loud_level_db > min_level_db` (:255) makes `cfs._calibrate_floor`'s `dataclasses.replace(cfg, min_level_db=max_band+8)` (cfs.py:876) raise `ValueError` at arm whenever any band exceeds −18 dBFS — `feedback_watch` would crash instead of arming at show level. ~60 inline literals justified by named corpus incidents.

**4. disc-minimal-delta (wt/disc-minimal-delta @ f74f953).** Honest, small, and its growth window n_g = ⌈4/(Δf·T)⌉ (:785-804) is the same physics as the winners' settle rule; cluster prominence, centroid `freq_hz`, the frozen-display guard (`frozen_eps_db`, :825-834) and the "override is provably redundant" argument are all worth having. It ranks fourth because two of its four lanes are wrong in kind, not in tuning. The step-memory arrival rule (`_is_step` :661, `_update_steps` :680-698, :751-764) declares any ≥9 dB rise completed in ≤3 frames "programme" *and holds the flag until the band returns to its pre-arrival level* — which a ring holding the band up never does — so every watch-mode howl that plateaus below the −6 dBFS CLIP lane is permanently invisible (A1 417 dB/s to −14: 0/3; A9 ring born inside a cymbal wash: 0/3; X11 0/3), a regression against the *current* detector, which catches A1/A9 via the override. The SUSTAINED lane cuts any family-less steady line ≥ −45 dBFS after 300/600 ms with no onset-history requirement (organ chord at arm 18 FP, whine −40 27 FP, and 8 FP across 12 genuinely unseen corpus seeds on X7/X8/X14/X16/X20 neighbourhoods), and CLIP at −6 ignores MUSICAL (loud whistle 23 FP). The probe lane never decides anything on the corpus (the claimed X23 EARLY is `reasons=('growth',)`), and the code defaults (`narrow_db` 12, `centroid_wander_bands` 0.75, `comove_db` 3, `growth_lf_periods` 2 at :205-233) differ from the documented/yaml values, so with the repo's current device.yaml the design silently degrades to 47/61 — the brief required it to work with the current yaml.

**5. disc-sequential-evidence (wt/disc-sequential-evidence @ f882837).** It detects the most (79/99 on the breakers; the *only* design that reports B2/Y3/A1-fast/A9/Y14, via its multi-frame and "2-frame rise stopping within 16 dB of FS" shape terms, and the only one that does not take the −40 dBFS at-arm bait — X7 then costs 1.6-6.5 s), and its cost-ratio derivation of the emit thresholds (≈20:1 watch, ≈4:1 ring-out, `emit_llr_*` :181) is the clearest statement of Q4 on the panel. It is last because it fails the first criterion on the corpus itself: 21 FP on hold-out seeds 4-9 of plain programme scenarios (llr crossing 3.0 by 0.03-0.2 nats), 2-10 FP on every analyser sweep but BQ, `analyser_attack_k 0.5` (:188, :554) is the simulator's own τ_a verbatim (CORPUS §7.1), the family-less held-note fixture had to be shortened to 80 frames because the design fires at frame 117, and absolute level is *positive* evidence (a −12 dBFS sine-lead solo is cut in 250 ms, A1 16 FP; a −3 dBFS family-less note on its first frame) while harmonics below −16 dBFS latch "music" for life (A2/A3 textbook howls never cut; A4 7.1 s from an undocumented emit-time harmonic veto at :1552-1561). Nine additive bounded bonuses with ~80 hand-set magnitudes is the weighted sum re-derived; no constant can be calibrated in isolation on the desk. Ring-out numbers describe an integration that does not exist (cfs.py unchanged, `cfg.mode` defaults to watch).

## 4. The brief's four questions, on all the evidence

**Q1 — Can a harmonic-structure test replace growth, and what does it cost in latency?** It replaces growth as the *music veto* and it must be a track-level test, not a peak-level one: partials present as peaks at the exact offsets (+10, +15.85, +20, +23.2 bands, ±0.5-0.6 band by centroid), within ~15-18 dB of the candidate, and **co-moving** (shared onset within the analyser rise time, correlated envelope) — otherwise dense chords veto real rings 25-40 % of frames (predicates iter data, X8/X12/X21) and two rings an octave apart are dismissed. That disposes of voice, bass, guitars, keys, saw pads, hum and speech in 1-3 frames — 80 % of the baseline's 948 FP. It cannot *confirm* feedback: absence of family is informative only above ~20-25 dB prominence (A §4.3), and the family-less programme set (organ flue, flute top register, whistle, sine lead/pad, 808, bells, HVAC whine) passes it in every design and is exactly the residual FP class of all five. So growth does not disappear; it changes job — from mandatory separator with a 6-60 dB/s window to *one of several* sufficient pieces of positive evidence, measured as the line's own rise net of common mode, at any rate, and **only after the band could physically have settled (≥ 1-1.5/Δf)** — that last clause, present in predicates and free-hand and absent in track-and-group's in-window LF behaviour, is what actually kills the M7 40/80 Hz cuts. Latency is then not set by the harmonic test (1 frame) but by K1 ≈ 5 frames of stationarity plus the time to see evidence: 50-250 ms for rings that ramp in view at ≥ 20 dB/s (free-hand median 100 ms, predicates+graft ≈ 150-200), 200-250 ms for a ≥ 30 dB established howl at arm, 6/R seconds for slow rings, one dwell (1.5 s) for anything the probe must resolve, and *never, passively,* for a 15-25 dB family-less plateau that was not seen to start — that case is a candidate for the human in watch and a probe target in ring_out.

**Q2 — Weighted sum or predicates?** Predicates, and the panel settled it empirically: the one design that re-derived a sum (sequential-evidence, nine bounded LLR terms) is the one that false-cut ordinary programme on merely re-seeded corpus scenarios (21 FP) and on every analyser variant, because a sum lets duration, level and "still no partials" jointly buy a cut that no single physical fact justifies, and because ~80 jointly-tuned magnitudes cannot be calibrated one at a time on a desk. The predicate designs failed too, but each failure is one named threshold with one physical story (−40 dBFS at-arm gate; 4 dB long-window rise; step memory) that can be moved or removed without retuning the rest, and every cut carries `reasons` the notch report can print. Keep a monotone confidence for the dashboard; never decide on it.

**Q3 — Is a frequency window a legitimate prior?** A fixed 250 Hz–8 kHz window is a correct statement about one rig (HPF'd SM58 into tops, no mic path to subs) and wrong for kick/tom mics into subs (35-120 Hz, X22 at 65 Hz), acoustic-guitar bodies through wedges (90-250 Hz, X17/S12), lav/lectern (150-800 Hz) and condensers (rings to 12 kHz; even M7's SM58 rang at 8.1 kHz on the window's edge) (L §3). More importantly the evidence says the window is **not what fixes M7**: predicates and free-hand both give 0 FP on every LF programme trap (S1/S7/X5/X15/S17/S19, B9/B10) with the floor at 40 Hz or absent, under the default, attack_k 1.0 and BQ analysers (verified here), because rises are only believed after the band's settle time and LF re-excitations restart the run; track-and-group, which relied on the edge, reproduced the 40 Hz cut the moment the edge moved. So: derive a per-session window from what the desk knows (min over open contributing channels of 0.7·f_hpf or a per-kind default; 160 Hz watch / 100 Hz ring_out defaults; 40 Hz on `lf_feedback_possible` or a kick/tom/acoustic-pickup channel on a bus with an LF path; top 12.5 kHz, 10 kHz if all open mics are dynamic), print the derivation in the report, and treat it as a cheap prior that removes candidates — never as the discriminator.

**Q4 — What to do when uncertain, and does it differ by mode?** Uncertain means: narrow, family-less, stationary, steady, not decaying, in window — and no positive evidence (not seen to rise after settle, not loud relative to the mix, not tier-A prominent at arm, no probe response). Passively that observation is a held organ/flute/whistle/sine note far more often than a howl, and the corpus's irreducible pairs (X4 vs X7 vs X20 vs S2a) prove no threshold separates them without encoding a level regularity — four of five designs encoded one (−40/−45 dBFS) and all four false-cut an organ note or a projector whine sounding at arm. Therefore: **watch** (human owns the fader, programme present, wrong cut ≈ 20× a late alert): publish the candidate at K1 ≈ 250 ms with its reasons, cut only on positive evidence, allow exactly one bounded exception — the tier-B one-shot −3 dB for a loud, popped, family-less, dead-stationary line (the cupped-mic howl), never deepened on the same evidence, self-disabling after one VERIFY-classified false cut — and promote a long-lived uncertain line only when it outlasts programme structure (P12) or the operator acks it. **ring_out** (server owns the gain, no audience, nobody on the fader, and a missed LF/quiet mode is what the procedure exists to find): resolve uncertainty *actively* — HOLD the raise, use every +1 dB step as a probe (two ≥ +2 dB/dB over-responses ⇒ cut before runaway; ≤ 1 dB/dB twice ⇒ STATIONARY/DRIVEN, session ignore-list, never cut), add the −1 dB reverse probe before cutting an ambiguous line, back the master off rather than sit under a growing howl when the budget is spent, and drop to watch thresholds if spectral flux at arm says programme is present (sequential-evidence's forced-ring-out run: 119 FP on music is what "no programme by contract" is worth when violated; predicates' `ringout_emit_moderate` on M2: 9 FP).

## 5. What the corpus still cannot tell us (carry into the M7 re-test)

1. **The analyser is a model.** τ_a = 0.5/Δf one-pole, Butterworth-3 skirts, dB-linear release, guessed peak-hold/gain semantics. The winners' settle rules survive k ∈ {0.32, 0.5, 1.0}, but an FFT-based bank that smears a 40 Hz tone over ±2-4 bands, window-shaped (sigmoid) rises, or LF skirts shallower than N=2 would change prominence ceilings, narrowness and the edge-tone centroid at once. §3's oscillator test closes this in 20 minutes; until then every LF number is provisional.
2. **Whether `/-prefs/rta/gain` offsets `/meters/15` and whether peak-hold reaches the stream.** Every absolute tier (LOUD, clip, tier B's floor) and the frozen-display flag depend on it; the gain +12/+24 sweeps (45-310 FP) show the exposure if cfs does not force and verify the prefs.
3. **Real programme micro-statistics.** Timbres are static tables, modulation is a sum of ≤ 5 slow sinusoids, band noise is independent across bands, beds are smooth humps. The margin under `rise_db` 6 (largest BASE-line swell on the corpus 3.6 dB) is a simulator fact; real flute/organ/whistle players, Leslie chorale, sidechain-pumped synths, brass/sax/bowed strings/accordion drones, distorted or deliberately fed-back guitar and crowd singing are absent. The breakers show every design cuts a family-less dB-linear swell; how often that occurs per hour of real programme is the unknown that decides whether watch mode is trusted.
4. **Probe visibility.** Over-response strength depends on `excite_coupling_db` and comb/band geometry (CORPUS §7.6); the corpus says ≥ 2 dB/dB only within ~3 dB of threshold with ±2 dB step noise under noise excitation. If the real desk shows less, `probe_min_hits` 2 within a 1.4 s window will not fire before runaway and ring_out falls back to RISE after crossing (still fine at +1 dB steps, but the pre-emptive cut and STATIONARY classification are then paper features). Reverberant multi-path loops (X21 class) have no clean comb at all and exist in one scenario.
5. **Plateau behaviour.** Real compressor/limiter plateaux pump 1-3 dB with the programme sharing the channel and overshoot on attack; `sag_db` 4, `arm_range_db` 6, `decay_db_per_s` 3 and the "steady" tests were set against ±0.3-0.5 dB multi-sine wander. A pumping plateau could read as "decaying" (missed re-emission) or restart runs.
6. **Base rates of the two irreducible classes in watch use**: fast howls to a sub-loud plateau (how often e ≥ 2 dB really happens from fader shoves/cupping — sets the value of tier B) and family-less lines sounding at the instant of arming (sets the cost of any at-arm shortcut). Neither is knowable offline; log them.
7. **Closed loop is idealised**: RBJ bell of guessed Q on a PRE insert, one-frame actuation, no bus dynamics between tap and insert, cfs's VERIFY/deepen/release/abort logic not exercised, no operator fighting the system, no two open mics with different τ, no autogain-on-during-ring, no RTA source switch mid-session, no frame bursts/duplicates. The "never deepen a wrong cut" fix (graft 1e) and the tier-B self-disable can only be validated against real VERIFY traces.
8. **Scoring generosity and sample size**: ±1 band / 1/6-oct attribution with 1 s look-back, EARLY credit 2 s (X23 passes for three designs only because of it), 3 tuning seeds + 3-6 hold-out, C-major random walks, two midpoint rings. "0 FP on the corpus" was necessary; the hold-out and sweep columns above are the closest thing to sufficient the panel produced, and only two designs cleared them.

## 4. The brief's four questions, given all the evidence

**Q1 — Can a harmonic-structure test replace growth outright? Latency?** No, and the panel proved it five different ways. Presence-as-peak at the exact offsets (+10, +15.85, +20, +23.2 bands, ±0.5-0.6 band, level within ~15-18 dB, co-moving or co-born) is an excellent single-frame *music veto* for voice, bass, guitar, piano, saw pads, hum and speech — it is what removed 80 % of the 948 baseline FPs in every design — but it is blind to the family-less class (808/sine sub, Hammond 8′, flute top register, whistles, sine leads, bells, HVAC whine: A §4.3), uninformative below ~20 dB prominence, produces coincidental vetoes under dense harmony unless partners must share history, and must be overridden near clip (odd partials of a howl). What replaces growth is not the harmonic test but the removal of any *required* term: line ∧ ¬family ∧ stationary (centroid ±0.25 band) ∧ steady/not-decaying ∧ in-window, plus one positive fact — own rise ≥6 dB net of common mode after the band could settle, loud/clip, established-at-arm, or probe over-response. Latency is then set by the stability window, not the harmonic check: K1 = 5 frames = 250 ms; established howl 200 ms after arm at any prominence ≥18; fast rings 50-250 ms (with back-fill, ≤100 ms); a ring at R dB/s that is neither loud nor at-arm costs ≈6/R s after it becomes trackable (7-15 dB/s → 400-850 ms, the honest residual of refusing to cut a steady family-less note); below ~3 dB/s only the probe (ring_out) or tier-B policy (watch) meets a 1 s budget.

**Q2 — Weighted sum or predicates?** Predicates, decisively — and now with an experiment behind it: the one design that summed evidence (bounded LLR terms, cost-derived threshold) re-created the exact pathology — surplus level bought a cut on a −12 dBFS sine lead while an onset penalty vetoed a textbook howl, the threshold was tuned to within 0.03 nats of seeds 1-3, and every perturbation (new seeds, any analyser variant) produced FPs. The predicate designs failed too, but each failure is one named threshold with a physical meaning (a skirt depth, a partial offset, 1/Δf, a doubling, a dBFS line) that can be moved, calibrated on the desk, unit-tested alone, and printed in the notch report as `reasons`. Confidence stays as a monotone display number.

**Q3 — Is a 250 Hz-8 kHz window a legitimate prior?** Legitimate per session, a hack as a constant, and — the corpus's most useful negative result — *not the fix for M7*. Real bounds: low edge = max(loudspeaker LF path, ≈0.7×channel HPF, mic far-field roll-off) → ~150-160 Hz for HPF'd dynamic vocals into tops, 100-125 Hz lav/lectern/condensers/wedges with un-HPF'd mics, ~80 Hz acoustic pickups, 35-40 Hz kick/tom/bass-cab into subs; high edge 10 kHz (all-dynamic) to 12.5 kHz (condensers); M7's own ring was 8.1 kHz, on the proposed edge. The winning design gets 0 FP on every LF programme scenario *with the window open to 40 Hz* because it refuses to score rises the analyser could have manufactured (settle ≈1.5/Δf) and restarts on LF re-excitation; the design that relied on the edge reproduced the 40/80 Hz cuts as soon as the edge moved (track-and-group B10/B11, S19 16 FP). So: ship the settle rule unconditionally, derive the window from `hpon/hpf/hpslope` + mic kind + `lf_path` (L §3.4), default 160 Hz-12.5 kHz watch / 63-100 Hz ring_out / 40 Hz on `lf_feedback_possible`, and print the window and its derivation in the session report.

**Q4 — Behaviour when uncertain; watch vs ring_out.** "Uncertain" has a precise meaning in the winner: MODERATE = every structural predicate holds and no positive evidence exists — and passively that state is *more often a note than a howl* (organ, flute, whistle, sine lead, a whine present at arm) while also containing the compressor-caught howl that arrived in two frames. Watch (human owns the fader, programme present, wrong cut audible/cumulative/budget-consuming): never cut on structure alone; publish `cfs.candidate` with reasons at ≤250 ms; cut immediately on tier A (clip, LOUD, ≥6 dB own rise, established ≥18 dB at arm); tier B (G7): loud-ish MODERATE surviving 600 ms → one −3 dB cut that VERIFY classifies, never deepened without tier-A evidence, released/ignore-listed on "drop ≈ bell". Ring_out (server owns the gain, no programme by contract, nobody on the fader): HOLD raising on any MODERATE; resolve actively — each +1 dB step is a probe, two over-responses (≥ step+2 dB, common mode flat) ⇒ cut, pre-emptively if still sub-threshold; ≤1 dB/dB twice ⇒ STATIONARY, reported, never cut; prefer a −1…−6 dB back-off over sitting under a growing uncertain line; never emit AT-ARM before a probe verdict unless LOUD/clip (G4); verify the no-programme contract at arm and fall back to watch policy if violated (G8). Both modes: force and restore the RTA prefs at arm, or none of the above is measuring the room.

## 5. What the corpus still cannot tell us (carry into the M7 re-test)

1. **The fast howl to a moderate plateau is undefended by every detector on the panel.** e ≥ 1.5-3 dB on a 5-11 ms loop → −60 to a −12…−25 dBFS compressor/limiter plateau in 2-3 frames (L §1.3-1.4: the normal watch case) is passively identical to a sine-lead note onset (C §7.10). Only G1 (≥3 back-filled frames, i.e. ≤ ~250 dB/s) and the cfs tier-B one-shot policy address it; above ~300 dB/s and below −20 dBFS it remains alert-only. Provoke it on the desk (cup the mic at the wedge) and confirm the candidate is published ≤250 ms and the tier-B path behaves.
2. **Solo family-less dB-linear swells are cut by every design** (flute/soprano messa di voce, sine pad, organ swell pedal, one channel's fader ride: Y1/Y2/Y9/Y12, A5/A6, B1). That envelope *is* the ring signature; passively irreducible within 300 ms. Frequency in real programme unknown (corpus timbres are static tables, C §7.3); mitigation is VERIFY-classify + no-deepen + release (G2/G7) and the calibration log of item 3 row 6.
3. **Absolute dBFS legs vs RTA gain/det** — 45-53 FP at +12 dB; whether `gain` reaches `/meters/15` is UNCONFIRMED. Until the oscillator test says otherwise, cfs must force gain 0 and the detector must treat a spectrum whose p95 sits above −20 dBFS at arm as `hot_spectrum` (flag, strict tier).
4. **The analyser itself** (C §7.1): all robustness shown is across *modelled* variants (U/BQ, N=2/3/5, release laws). A short-FFT implementation would smear LF tones over ±2-4 bands and void narrowness/centroid/prominence below ~300 Hz; peak-hold reaching the stream would void steadiness. The §3 oscillator test is a precondition for trusting any LF verdict, including "0 FP with the window at 40 Hz".
5. **Probe statistics** rest on three scenarios with guessed `excite_coupling` and an idealised comb (C §7.6); the 2 dB × 2 hits rule, 1.4 s window and STATIONARY veto are unvalidated against real room noise, real spill and reverberant multi-delay loops (one scenario, X21). Also untested: >32 steps (F2), back-off probes, two open mics with different τ.
6. **The at-arm irreducible pair** (X7 −30 vs held organ note vs −36…−50 whine vs S2a −8): the winner separates them with `arm_line_min_level_db −40` + 18 dB + 0.8 s, which is a corpus regularity (Y8 miss below 18 dB, Y9 FP at −36). In ring_out G4 removes it; in watch it will misfire in some room — report AT-ARM cuts distinctly so the operator can veto, and calibrate per venue from the 2 s arm baseline.
7. **Real spectral flux**: coincidental-partial veto rates, narrowness under dense transient partials, masking durations (S3/X9-type lateness) — corpus FP pressure is a lower bound and latency an optimistic one (C §7.2). Hold-out FPs in every design were near-sine notes re-born on occupied bands; expect more of that live.
8. **Closed-loop policy is unvalidated** (C §7.8): GEQ Q, PRE-insert depth at the tap, VERIFY under real decay ballistics, deepen/release/budget accounting, flanking-pair cuts for midpoint rings (X9 sits between GEQ 500/630), hop → new budget line, and the L §1.2 prediction that after each notch the next candidate rings at essentially the same master setting (budget exhaustion in 3-6 dB). The re-test should log `first_feedback_master_db` per notch and stop when successive values differ by <1 dB.
9. **Operational cases absent from the corpus** (C §7.12): operator riding a fader continuously through a ring in watch (Y9-type common-mode that is *not* spectrum-wide), autogain left on, RTA source switched mid-session, frame bursts/duplicates, two simultaneous rings from two mics, a limiter that pumps the plateau 1-3 dB with programme (steadiness/sag thresholds).
10. **Scoring generosity**: EARLY credit (X23 passes partly on −1.25 s pre-emptive emissions; with `EARLY_CREDIT_S=0` several designs lose a scenario) and ±1-band attribution mean reported latencies are best-case; judge the re-test on wall-clock from audible onset to GEQ write, from the logs.

## 1. Architecture (per frame; O(bands) + O(peaks·30) lookups; measured 0.29 ms mean, 0.74 ms p99, 2.8 ms max per frame, CPython 3.13, 8 scenarios / 2320 frames, ≤ 11 live tracks)

1. **Peaks** (`_find_peaks` L575): per band `prominence = level − median(±3)` (L563) and, new, a *cluster prominence* =
   power(b−1..b+1) − median(bands ±2..±4); the larger counts, so a line half-way between centres (−3/−3 dB, S10/X9) is not 3 dB
   short [A §3, C §2 item 11]. A local max with prominence ≥ `peak_floor_db` 6 is a peak. Per peak: **centroid** = vertex of the
   parabola through the dB levels of b−1,b,b+1, clamped ±0.5 (edge tone → b+0.5 from either side ⇒ one object; interior offsets
   compressed ~25 %, harmless because harmonic partners share the compression); **cluster power** cp (offset- and
   vibrato-invariant, [A §4.4]); **narrowness** = level − max(level at ±2) [N P1, A §3(a)].
2. **Tracks** (`Candidate`, `_associate` L876): peaks ≥ `track_floor_db` 8 dB start a track — 4 dB under the emission
   threshold, so a ring already has 5–15 frames of history when it becomes "visible". Association by centroid: pass 1 radius
   `assoc_tol_bands` 0.6; pass 2 radius 0.85 only for a track whose own centroid has been swinging (vibrato); pass 3 lets an
   unmatched track claim a peak anywhere inside the span it swung over recently (±0.35). A peak ≥ 0.35 band away that is ≥ 6 dB
   above the track's last cluster power (or turns up after the track went missing ≥ 2 frames) is a **new object** and starts its
   own track (X16 lesson: a wedge ring born a band away from a bass partial must not inherit the partial's history). Tracks
   survive `max_gap_frames` 5 masked frames (crash/snare, speech partials hopping over a ring). Per track, bounded histories
   (48) of centroid, peak-band level, cp, cp lower envelope (3-frame min: removes additive hits), prominence, narrowness,
   spectrum reference, family flag; and a back-fill of the band's last 16 frames (cluster power and peak-band level) taken at
   birth from a frame ring buffer.
3. **Onset class** at frame 3 of a track (`_classify_onset` L961), from back-fill + first 3 frames of the peak-band level:
   `at_arm` (born in the first 4 frames: no onset information); `adult` (arrived gliding ≥ 0.4 band over its first 3 frames, or
   reached within 1.5 dB of its 3-frame max within ≤ 2 frames of leaving the floor [floor = median of the back-fill; "left" =
   +min(6, 0.35·rise)]: an instrument note, or an LF onset smeared by the analyser — a one-pole at τ_a still puts > 65 % of the
   dB rise in the first two frames [A §1 table, L §1.6]); `ramp` (best run of ≥ 3 consecutive increments ≥ 0.4 dB, ≥ 4 dB total,
   second-half mean ≥ 0.5 × first-half, no single/pair of increments carrying the rise); else `slow`.
4. **Grouping** (`_group` L712, `_compatible` L652, `_coborn` L607, `_cogrowing` L631, `_coramping` L616). For each peak P
   and hypothesis "P is harmonic m of f0=f/m", m∈{1..5}, partials k∈{1..6}\{m} are predicted at centroid+10·log2(k/m) bands
   [A §4.1] and looked up among *peaks* within `harmonic_tol_bands` 0.3 (36 ¢): a partial above P must be within
   `partner_window_db` 12 dB of P's level (howl distortion products are ≥ 12–30 dB down [L §2.2]; X11's H3 at −14 dB is thereby
   not a partner), one below may be up to `sub_window_db` 15 dB quieter (HPF'd bass H1 under its H2 [A §4.2]); m ≥ 4 only if k=1
   is present and ≥ 10 dB louder (otherwise chords "explain" too many positions by chance — X7's ring sat 36 ¢ from 5×E4).
   A partner counts only if its track is **compatible** with P's: born within 3 frames (or both at arm) *and not diverged since*
   (level difference changed ≤ 5 dB — S3 seed 1: an organ C6 struck in the very frame the 3×C6 ring became trackable; the
   ring then out-climbed it by 30 dB); or ≥ 6 frame-aligned common samples whose envelopes correlate (both ranges ≥ 2.5 dB,
   Pearson ≥ 0.85, difference drift ≤ 4 dB: co-swell/co-decay) or whose increments correlate (ρ ≥ 0.6, both sd ≥ 0.3 dB:
   vibrato through the skirts, tremolo); or < 6 common samples (too young to judge: benefit of the doubt — a partial crosses the
   tracking floor later than its fundamental in a slow swell or through a slower LF band). **Family** := some hypothesis has ≥ 2
   partners, or exactly 1 partner within 0.2 band of the exact position that is *co-born* (±2 frames, or both at arm) or
   *co-growing* (≥ 5 common frames both ≥ 8 dB prominent, both risen ≥ 3 dB, level-difference range ≤ 0.8 dB). Two rings of one
   rig at ×2 (X10, S11b ×2.016) are born 6–12 frames apart and their difference drifts at |R1−R2| = 10–12 dB/s, so they are
   neither; the 8′+4′ organ pair (S6/S6b/X12), flute+H2, pad H1+H2 are. If the single exact-position partner is < 5 frames old,
   or is itself climbing (≥ 3 dB/8 frames) at a level within 10 dB of P, P is marked **pending**: growth-based cuts are *held*
   (not vetoed) until the relation resolves — this absorbs the chorused synth pad (S21), whose partials beat at k× the detune
   rate and therefore fail every envelope test, at a cost of ≤ 250 ms on the one S11b seed where the second ring is born inside
   the first one's decision window.
5. **Behaviour** (windowed, in `_decide` L1247): `family` = family flag on ≥ 40 % of the last 20 frames, or lineage (≥ 6 of
   10 once; fades 2 s after the last family frame — S5's guitar note decaying to a sine is meanwhile `adult` and `decaying`);
   `stationary_k` (`_stationary` L1165) = over the last k centroids, outliers beyond 0.35 band ≤ 25 %, **MAD ≤ 0.15 band**
   (±25 ¢ vibrato shows; a neighbour partial yanking the interpolation for a frame does not) and last within 0.75 of the
   median; `glide` = birth glide (first 15 frames) or a window-to-window median step > 0.5 band (legato note change / hop);
   `comoving` (`_comoving` L1181) = over 12 frames, on frames where the reference (median of bands 25..85) is within 4 dB of its
   window floor (drum hits excluded), reference range ≥ 1.0 dB, regression of the cp lower envelope on the reference has slope
   0.6..1.5 and residual ≤ 0.35·sd+0.15 (fader/common-mode move [A §5]; also set when ≥ 2 other tracks ramp at 0.5–2× this
   one's rate = ensemble swell/crescendo, rings start alone [A §5]); `decaying` = lower-envelope LS slope ≤ −3 dB/s over 12
   frames with ≥ 1 dB drop (plucked/struck notes 3–15 dB/s [L §2.1]; a killed ring; S15's 3.75 dB/s display release);
   `frozen` = peak-band value bit-identical on ≥ 6 of the last 7 steps (peak-hold display, S16 — a live line through a real
   analyser never repeats to 1/256 dB; exempt at clip); `steady` = lower-envelope range ≤ 2.0 dB over 15 frames; `slow_rise` =
   LS over the last 16–30 lower-envelope samples: 0.8–8 dB/s, ≥ 3 dB, residual ≤ 0.8 dB, not comoving (S13/X8 marginal loops
   [L §1.3 row e=0.1]); **ramp** (`_ramp_stats` L1019, cached per frame) = better of (a) the run of per-frame increments ≥ 0.4 dB
   ending now (one dip allowed, gaps time-normalised, leading noise blips trimmed) and (b) an LS line over the last 6..16 samples
   (slope ≥ 6 dB/s, rise ≥ 4 dB, residual ≤ max(0.6, 0.06·rise) [0.12·rise for ≥ 12 samples at ≥ 12 dB/s], halves' slopes
   within 0.5–2.5×, no increment > half the rise, last increment ≥ 30 % of the fitted per-frame rate) → (n_inc, rise, slope,
   still, ref_floor_change, linear, strict); `ramp_ok` additionally needs the reference's *lower envelope* not to have risen by
   more than half the line's rise [N P4] and the line's own lower envelope to climb at ≥ 40 % of the fitted rate (vibrato
   riding the skirts cannot fake it); `fast` (`_fast_ramp` L1139) = peak-band level (with back-fill) rose ≥ 15 dB in ≥ 3
   consecutive steps of ≥ 5 dB/frame, none > half the rise, not fallen back; **probe** (`_probe` L1216, ring_out only): for
   each announced master step the track lived through, median cp over [t+0.2, t+0.65] minus median over [t−0.45, t) minus the
   step (sign-aware) → `probe_strong` (≥ 4 dB once or ≥ 2 dB with ≥ 1.2 dB the step before), `probe_mild` (≥ 1 dB),
   `probe_linear` (|·| ≤ 0.8 on the last steps) [L §1.5, A §0.3].
   **Re-qualification**: ≥ 6 linear increments and ≥ 8 dB of solitary growth turn an `adult/slow/at_arm` track into `ramp` and
   truncate its family record to the last 3 frames; ≥ 0.8 s of solitary dB-linear creep turns `adult` into `slow` (a chord
   tone's/kick's band taken over by a ring: X12, X17, X19, X21, X22, X8).

## 2. Decision logic (L1428–1462) — predicates, no weighted sum [brief Q2]

Gate (`base`): centroid inside the window [c(f_low)−0.5, c(f_high)+0.5]; level ≥ `level_floor_db` −72; visible = prominence
reached `prominence_db` 12 within the last 5 frames and is ≥ 9 now; narrow (≥ 9 dB on ≥ half of the last 5 frames); age ≥
`persistence_frames` 3; not family; stationary over 5; not frozen. `settled` = gate ∧ no glide.

* **A:clip** — level ≥ `clip_level_db` −3 (family ignored: a howl at full scale grows odd harmonics [L §2.2, A §4.3]; the
  corpus renders a line pinned at 0.0 as −1.7).
* **A:loud** — level ≥ `loud_level_db` −10 for 5 frames, settled, not moving [L §4.3 tier A]. (Loudest programme line in the
  corpus: crowd whistle −17; legacy held-note test peaks at −11.9.)
* **A:ramp** — `ramp_strong` ∧ stationary over the ramp's own span ∧ no family flag now ∧ no partner pending, where
  ramp_strong = ramp_ok ∧ [(n_inc ≥ 6 ∧ still rising) ∨ (rise ≥ 24 ∧ n_inc ≥ 4) ∨ (n_inc ≥ 4 ∧ still ∧ strictly linear ∧ rate ≤ 45
  dB/s ∧ prominence ≥ 14)] ∨ fast; an `adult`-born track needs rise ≥ 10 dB. Physical content: exponential regeneration is
  linear in dB at e/τ [L §1.3]; ≥ 300 ms of it, or ≥ 24 dB of it, or ≥ 200 ms of it slower than any note attack and not
  decelerating (the analyser's step response decelerates [L §1.6]), or ≥ 150 ms at ≥ 100 dB/s in even steps (a 40–80 ms note
  attack makes ≤ 2 steps) — while nothing else rose (reference floor) and no partial co-onset.
* **A:probe** — probe_strong, settled (ring_out; also admits an `adult` steady line after 15 frames).
* **A:prominent** — onset `at_arm`, prominence ≥ `strong_prominence_db` 18 now and as 5-frame median, steady, level ≥
  `min_level_db`, settled, not decaying, not probe-linear: the established ring at arm (S2a/b/c, X7, X12 episode 1) in 250 ms
  [N "established ring at arm is emitted 250 ms after arming"].
* **B** — after `watch_confirm_frames` 15 clean frames (stationary over 15, not moving/comoving/decaying/glide/probe-linear),
  onset ∈ {at_arm, slow, ramp} and one of: slow_rise; ramp_ok; steady ∧ level ≥ min_level ∧ (prominence median ≥ 18 [watch] |
  in ring_out: probe_mild on the last step, or no steps announced).
* `adult` onset + not loud/clip ⇒ never cut in watch (published as candidate; `reasons` says why). In ring_out only the probe
  can promote it.
* Emission: per-track cooldown `cooldown_s`, band cooldown ±`band_tolerance`; no re-emission while `decaying` (VERIFY sees the
  cut work; a 16 s RTA release is not re-cut: S15 0 TAIL). `Detection.freq_hz` is interpolated from the window-median centroid
  (X9's 525 Hz between GEQ 500/630 reports 522–526 Hz), `band` = nearest RTA band, `reasons` lists tier, onset, prominence,
  narrowness, ramp figures, probe response. `confidence` is a monotone display figure (≥ threshold iff a tier fired).

What is deliberately NOT used: absolute growth window/onset guard (growth is evidence, never required, never capped [N]);
`min_level_db` as a global gate (it gates only steady no-growth lines: X20's −50 dBFS whine is the corpus's own
"irreducible_passive"; a line seen to grow is judged from −72); P5 "new energy over a per-band baseline" [N] — **rejected**: an
established ring at arm IS the baseline, and X7/S2 must be cut; the information P5 wanted is carried by the onset class instead.

## 2. Decision logic in full (src/x32mcp/detector.py; thresholds = device.yaml values)

Per frame (`feed`, l.890): `ref` = median of bands 25..85 (spectrum reference, [A §5]); per-band step memory updated (`_update_steps` l.680); cluster levels cached for co-movement; prominences.

**Q. Qualification** (l.900-909). Band i qualifies iff inside the window (Q4), `level ≥ min_level_db` (−80: numeric floor only), `narrowness ≥ narrow_db` (10 dB) and (`prominence ≥ 12` or `cluster_prominence ≥ 12`).
* prominence = level − median(±3) (unchanged, DESIGN §12). cluster prominence = powersum(i−1..i+1) − median(i±2..±4) (l.561): a line between two centres reads −3/−3 dB [A §3]; this is also the corpus's visibility definition [C §1].
* narrowness (l.534) = level − 2nd loudest of {i±2, i±3}. Physics [A §3, N P1]: a lone sinusoid clears ≥ 24 dB at ±2 on N ≥ 2 skirts; formant humps / cymbal wash / PA ripple are ≥ 3 bands wide. The loudest of the four is forgiven because one unrelated partial two bands away is common and says nothing about this line's width (X9, X11, M3 otherwise never qualify). 10 not 12: it is an order statistic on a ±1–4 dB floor, 2 dB under the median-based prominence (S11a/S10 data).
* Runs of adjacent qualifying bands → loudest band = peak (unchanged); each peak gets a power centroid over b−1..b+1 (l.549).
**Q4 window** (`DetectorConfig.window_hz` l.306; [L §3.3-3.4], [A §4.5]): watch 160 Hz–12.5 kHz; ringout 100 Hz–12.5 kHz; `lf_feedback_possible` → 40 Hz. Derivation: loop gain of a far-field vocal mic into tops is 20–30 dB down below ~150 Hz (mic roll-off × HPF 0.7·fc × box cut-off) so in watch (programme present, LF = where programme energy is highest) LF lines are programme; ring_out has no programme by contract and exists to find modes → wider; kick/tom/acoustic pickups near subs ring at 40–200 Hz → operator/cfs opt-in. 12.5 kHz: condensers ring to 10–12 kHz, M7's SM58 rang at 8.1 kHz. cfs may narrow further (0.7 × lowest open HPF) — hook is the `window_lo_hz` key; not wired to the patch sheet in this change.

**S. Step memory** (`_is_step` l.661, `_update_steps` l.680; per band, independent of qualification). Over six consecutive levels l0..l5 of a band: an *arrival* is a rise ≥ `step_db` (9 dB) confined to k ≤ `step_max_frames` (3) frames ending at l4 with the frame before the span and the frame after (l4→l5) each < `step_continue_frac` (0.3) of it. The band stores the arrival level and the pre-arrival level and stays "alive" until its level is back within `step_release_db` (3 dB) of the pre-arrival level (the release tail of a note, however long RTA decay stretches it, is still that note: S15). Physics [L §1.3, §1.6], [A §1]: programme above ~300 Hz reaches full level within one frame (+ one partially integrated frame); portamento/scoops take 80–150 ms [A §4.2]; a loop grows through every level at e/τ dB/s and even at 18 dB/frame (360 dB/s) rises on ≥ 4 consecutive frames for any plateau ≥ 30 dB above its seed, so it is never an arrival. There is no dB/s cap anywhere. A candidate is `step_onset` while any band within 0.8 of its centroid is alive (l.752-766; 0.8: a line between centres owns both bands, a neighbour's arrival one band from a centred line is not this line's onset — M3/X8); the flag clears when no band within 1.0 is alive (hysteresis for vibrato on an edge, X6). When an arrival lands inside the growth window the window restarts after it (judge what follows an arrival, never the arrival) and any open probe measurement is void. When a track starts, the band's last `history_frames` (8) levels seed the growth window (`_seed_history`), so an onset that completed before qualification is still in view (X11/X16-type late qualification, S3 crash masking).

**F. Harmonic family** (`_family` l.623, `_partial_at` l.606, `_comoves` l.595). Offsets 10·log2 k: H2 +10, H3 +15.85, H4 +20, H5 +23.22 bands [A §4.1]. Partial present at fractional position p iff a local-maximum band q within `harmonic_tol_bands` (0.5) of p by centroid has prominence ≥ max(`harmonic_presence_db` 6, own prominence − `harmonic_rel_db` 18) [N P2 "presence as peak", L §2.2 "within ~15 dB"], and **co-moves**: range of (C_q − C_b) over the frames since the candidate's birth (≤ `comove_frames` 6; < 3 frames ⇒ accept) ≤ `comove_db` (5 dB) [L P1 "envelope co-moves"; A §4.4: cluster power is vibrato-immune; 5 dB admits chorus/ensemble beating ±3 dB (S21) and piano partials decaying at different rates, rejects syllables/notes pulsing next to a steady ring (X9, X7)]. Verdict per frame:
* **family (2)**: ≥ `family_min_partials` (2) of H2..H5 present; or the line is partial k of a lower present line that owns another partial j≠k, where the lower line is plausibly the fundamental for level: the candidate may exceed it by `parent_excess_low_db` (8) for k = 2,3 (open vowels, bass whose H1 the PA high-passed [A §4.2]) but only `parent_excess_high_db` (2) for k = 4,5 (no source's 4th/5th partial towers over its fundamental; X7's ring sits 35 c from organ E4×5 and 4 dB above E4 — a coincidence, not a partial).
* **pair (1)**: exactly one H2/H3 within `single_partial_rel_db` (15 dB) of the line's *level* (organ 8'+4' −4 dB, flute −15; a clipping howl's H2 ≤ −20, H3 −10…−17 and only near clip [L §2.2]), within `single_partial_tol_bands` (0.35) and co-moving within `single_partial_comove_db` (1.5); or such a line exactly an octave *below* (b is its H2: "no subharmonics ever" [L §2.2]). Two coexisting rings ×2.00/×2.02 apart (X10, S11b, < 1 % of events [L §2.3]) form at most a pair and, growing at their own e/τ, fail the 1.5 dB co-movement while either grows.
* Candidate keeps the last `family_window_frames` (12) verdicts and a lifetime count.
**MUSICAL** (l.800-812): strict := family on ≥ `family_veto_frac` (0.4) of the window, or centroid range over the sustain window > `centroid_wander_bands` (0.35; a ring's centroid sd < 0.05 band, vibrato ±30 c ≈ ±0.25 band [A §4.3 v, §4.4]); loose := pair on ≥ 0.4 of the window or pair/family on ≥ `family_lifetime_frac` (0.5) of the track's life (a held chord tone keeps its partners for life while partners' bands get shared/unshared by other chord tones; a ring's coincidences are rarer). A line ≥ `clip_level_db` is never MUSICAL.

**G. GROWTH lane** (l.782-798) — positive evidence, never required. Window = candidate levels since the last restart (drop > `monotonic_tolerance_db` 1 dB, or an arrival inside it), n ≥ n_g(band) = max(`growth_min_frames` 3, ⌈`growth_lf_periods` 4 /(Δf·T)⌉), Δf = 0.0693 f: 3 frames ≥ 400 Hz, 5 @ 250 Hz, 8 @ 160 Hz, 11 @ 110 Hz, 19 @ 63 Hz [A §1 table; L §1.6: any filter resolving Δf needs ≥ 1/Δf; 4/Δf puts a smeared onset in the first half of the window]. Require: LS slope of level AND of (level − ref) ≥ `growth_min_db_per_s` (6); rise of both ≥ `growth_rise_db` (6 dB) [common-mode/fader moves have no corrected rise, S6/X12a; a steady line over a fading mix has no absolute rise, S20]; two largest single-frame increments ≤ `growth_step_share` (0.7) × rise and ≥ `growth_min_steps` (3) increments each ≥ `growth_step_min_share` (0.5) × mean increment (a continuous ramp: a partially integrated first frame, a neighbour note's arrival or a vibrato swing are one or two jumps — the S3/S8/X3/X4 baseline FP mechanism [A §0.1]); later-half share ≥ `growth_late_share` (0.25) (an analyser-limited step decelerates: last 3 dB at 6–26 dB/s [L §1.6 table]; a loop's ramp is dB-linear [L §1.3]). Blocked by strict MUSICAL, and deferred while the current frame shows a family or the track is younger than `sustain_frames` and has shown a family (swelling pads surface high partials first, S21; voiced syllables glide, S22). No upper slope bound: M1 200 dB/s, X16 240, S9 115, X12b 100 are caught at 98–250 ms by growth or clip.

**P. SUSTAINED lane** (l.814-826) — the plateau lane that replaces the override. Require now: not `step_onset`; wander ≤ 0.35; current level ≥ max(last `sustain_frames`) − `sustain_drop_db` (3 dB) (plucked/struck notes decay 3–15 dB/s [L §2.1]; an intact loop does not [L §1.4]); not frozen (fewer than half the intervals in the window move by > `frozen_eps_db` 0.002 dB ⇒ RTA peak-hold display, S16 [L §0, A §2]); narrow; `level ≥ sustain_min_level_db` (−45); and age ≥ `sustain_frames` (6 = 300 ms) if max(prominence, cluster prominence) ≥ `sustain_strong_prominence_db` (18) else `sustain_moderate_frames` (12 = 600 ms) [A §4.3: below ~20 dB absence of family proves little; N: MODERATE waits K2 = 12]. Blocked by loose MUSICAL. `sustain_min_level_db` is the one absolute level left and it is confined to this lane on purpose: with no onset and no growth observed, level is the only passive evidence separating X20's −50 dBFS whine (present before arm, no family) from X7's −30 dBFS compressor-held howl [C §7.10]; −45 is the M7 number and is honest about being one; ring_out resolves quieter lines with the probe, growth has no level gate at all (X23's modes are caught from −60).

**C. CLIP lane**: `level ≥ clip_level_db` (−6) on `persistence_frames` consecutive frames and narrow ⇒ emit whatever the family/onset [L §1.4(3), A §1 "treat level ≥ −0.5 as its own predicate"; −6 leaves room for a limiter ceiling under 0 dBFS and a howl arriving in 2 frames at > 400 dB/s]. Programme lines at −6 dBFS per 1/10-oct band do not occur at a sanely staged pre-fader tap (X16's loud band peaks at −13); −13 would catch X11 and FP on X16 (measured, §4).

**R. PROBE lane** (ring-out active probe; `note_gain_step` l.875, l.828-835): at each server master step every candidate stores base = median(recent levels) − ref; a candidate not MUSICAL, with no arrival since the step, whose min(last 3 levels) − ref exceeds base + step + `probe_excess_db` (2 dB) within `probe_window_s` (1.2 s) scores a hit; `probe_confirmations` (2) hits ⇒ emit "probe" [L §1.5: ≤ 1 dB/dB for anything not regenerating, ≥ +3 dB/dB within 4 dB of threshold; C §2.3: with noise excitation the over-response is ≥ 2 dB/dB only within ~3 dB and noisy ⇒ two confirmations, 3-frame hold]. Newcomers born inside the window take base = min of their seeded history (a line that *appears* after a step is the strongest response). Result: X23 mode 1 caught 1.15 s *before* onset (EARLY), S6b/M2 unchanged, 0 probe FPs on organ programme.

**Verdict** (l.838-851): frames ≥ `persistence_frames` (3) and (CLIP ∨ (GROWTH ∧ ¬strict ∧ ¬fam_wait) ∨ (SUSTAINED ∧ ¬MUSICAL) ∨ (PROBE ∧ ¬MUSICAL)) and not in cooldown (±1 band, 1 s). `reasons` records the lanes plus `step`, `frozen`, `family`, `pair`, `wander`, `sustained@arm`. Confidence (dashboard only) = normalised 0.3·min(1,P/24) + 0.2·min(1,frames/6) + 0.5·(1 if verdict else ½·growth score), clamped to ≥ 0.7 iff verdict.

## 1. Decision logic

### 1.1 Shape of the procedure
Every narrow spectral line ≥ `track_prominence_db` (6 dB) over the ±3 median is tracked as a `Candidate` = one hypothesis
test H_F ("self-oscillating loop") vs H_P ("programme / stationary environmental line"). Per frame the detector measures
nine bounded evidence terms and sums them into `llr` (nats, clamped to [−6, +8]):

    llr = prior + narrow + family + stable + steady + shape + level + persist + probe          (detector.py:954-1345)

Three-way output per candidate per frame (`Candidate.state`):
* `llr ≥ emit_llr` → **EMIT** (a `Detection`; re-emitted once per `cooldown_s` only while the line is undiminished or regrowing — a working cut is never deepened, detector.py:1574-1577);
* `llr ≤ dismiss_llr` → **DISMISSED-AS-MUSICAL** (published, never emitted; evidence keeps flowing, so a loop that later takes over the band of a chord tone can climb out);
* otherwise **KEEP WATCHING** (published as `cfs.candidate` with the full `terms` breakdown and `reasons`).

Why sums of bounded, saturating terms rather than a running product of per-frame likelihoods: successive observations of "still stable / still steady / still no partials" on the same line are almost perfectly correlated, so counting them per frame would let *duration* masquerade as *evidence* — precisely the "override at ≥ 6 frames" failure generalised. Each term is the log-likelihood ratio of the *state* of that feature given everything seen so far, saturating at the value the physics supports; the genuinely sequential terms (growth per frame, probe per step, programme boundaries survived) accumulate. The per-frame increment of `llr` is the innovation. This keeps the SPRT reading (thresholds from costs, §1.4) while being honest about dependence.

### 1.2 Tracking mechanics (what a "line" is) — detector.py:632-950, 1438-1540
* **Cluster** = bands within ±1.6 of the line's previous sub-band position inside b−2..b+2: power sum (`cl_db`) and power-weighted centroid. Anchoring keeps an edge tone's pair fixed while its loudest band alternates ([A §3]; S10/X9 sit on an edge). `freq_hz` is the centroid frequency (GEQ choice, [L §2.1]).
* **Intrusion**: a programme partial landing in b±1 lifts that skirt while the peak band stays on its own trajectory (predicted from its last two increments); real FM moves energy *out* of the peak (anti-correlated, [A §4.4]). Intruded frames keep the previous centroid and use the peak-band increment (detector.py:884-897).
* **Masking**: when the ±2..±4 neighbourhood floor jumps ≥ 4 dB more than the spectrum reference did *and* the line is buried (< prominence_db+3 over it), the frame is skipped — not scored either way — and a track without a peak is kept alive up to 12 frames instead of dying (S3/S13/S14, [A §4.2 cymbals]).
* **Re-arm** (detector.py:787-843, 905-921): a ≥ 8 dB newcomer within ≤ 2 frames on top of an established weaker line that was not itself rising, or a line that decayed ≥ 12 dB and climbs ≥ 6 dB again, is a *new event*: family/vibrato/onset/growth lineage belonged to the previous occupant (speech partial that seeded the 525 Hz mode in X9, the choir partial under the 642 Hz mode in X21, the bass partial a band under the 318 Hz wedge ring in X16). FM cannot fake the jump: vibrato swings single bands by up to 10 dB but the ±1 power sum by ≤ 0.3 dB [A §4.4].
* **Association**: a peak within `band_tolerance`+0.55 of the centroid continues the track, except a ≥ 6 dB louder peak ≥ 0.7 band away (a new line, not a hop — a hop keeps its level, [L §2.3]).
* **Spectrum reference** `ref` = 3-frame median of the median of bands 25..85 (τ_a < 1 frame there): master/common-mode moves cancel in every trajectory feature ([A §5]); a `ref` jump ≥ 3 dB marks a common-mode step.

### 1.4 Thresholds from the cost asymmetry (brief Q4) — `emit_llr_*`, `dismiss_llr_*`
Bayes: act when posterior odds F:P exceed C_FP / C_FN(one decision epoch ≈ 300 ms).
* **watch** (human on the fader, programme present): C_FP = a −3 dB × ⅓-octave dip on running programme for the rest of the show (cuts-only), possibly deepened to −9 by re-detection, 1 of 6 budget lines, and — M7 — the budget the real event needed [L §4.2, HANDOVER §4b] ≈ 10 units; C_FN = 300 ms more of a howl that a present human catches in 1–3 s anyway ≈ 0.5 → odds 20:1 → **emit at +3.0 nats**; dismiss at −2.5 (revocable).
* **ring-out** (server owns the gain, no programme by contract, nobody on the fader): C_FP = a wrong report line + a budget line, no audience ≈ 3; C_FN = an unattended howl growing e/τ per second until abort/back-off ≈ 3 per epoch → ≈ 1:1, plus the option value of one more frame of fast-arriving evidence → **emit at +1.4 (≈ 4:1)**; dismiss at −3.5. And because the server owns the gain, *uncertain and not loud ⇒ wait for the next step's probe* rather than cut on structure alone (detector.py:1554-1558) [L §4.3].
* Both clamp to [−6, +8] so any state can be left again. `confidence` for the dashboard is a monotone map of `llr` that reads ≥ `confidence_threshold` exactly when EMIT fires.

### 1.5 Graceful degradation
Every term returns 0 when it cannot be measured: partial positions beyond band 99 (≥ 5 kHz: family rests on H2 or is neutral), ±2/±4 neighbours missing at spectrum edges (narrow/cluster prominence neutral), fewer than 5 samples (stable/steady neutral — the first 200 ms of a line are decided by prior + level + shape + family only), masked frames (nothing), frozen display (steady/persist/slow-growth withheld, `analyser_suspect` set for `cfs` to report ANALYSER_MISCONFIGURED), no `note_gain_step` calls (probe 0 — watch never depends on it). LF bands degrade *by physics*: gw → 0.17 at 39 Hz and minrun 11 frames, so an LF loop is carried by probe (ring-out), prior opt-in, family absence and eventually long linear growth — 1.0–1.2 s at 65 Hz (X22), §3.

## 1. Decision logic in full (`src/x32mcp/detector.py`; defaults = `device.yaml detector:`; K1 = 5 frames = 250 ms)

### 1.1 Per frame, whole spectrum — `feed()` :1163, `_lines()` :669, `_cm()` :626 (O(bands·k), k = 3)
* `prom[i]` = level − median(±3 neighbours) (unchanged definition).
* **Line** = local maximum with prom ≥ `track_prominence_db` 6. Cluster = peak + a neighbour within `cluster_merge_db` 6 dB (a tone between two centres reads −3/−3 dB [A §3]; edge vibrato alternates the louder band [A §4.4]). Per line: cluster power, **cluster prominence** (cluster power over the median of the six bands ±2..±4 beyond it — the ground truth's "cluster" measure), **narrowness** = peak − max(the two bands just outside the cluster's skirts), and the **centroid** = power-weighted position of peak±1 *after removing the local floor* (sub-band frequency to ~±0.1 band; the raw centroid of a weak line is dragged by the bed and broke tracks).
* **Common-mode reference** (`_cm`): spectra of the last 96 frames are kept; the common-mode change between two frames is the MEDIAN of the per-band changes of bands 25..85 that sit ≥ `ref_signal_db` 6 above the frame's 10th-percentile floor at both instants, excluding the line's own ±4. A fader/autogain/master step moves every signal band equally; a chord, crash or one growing line moves a minority [A §5]. ## 1. What the detector models, and why it is not a score

The old `0.3·prominence + 0.2·persistence + 0.5·growth ≥ 0.7` failed on the desk both ways for one structural reason [N 1-2, A §0]: a sum lets surplus in one physical requirement buy a deficit in another, and its one informative term (growth) is manufactured by the analyser at LF (a 1/10-octave band cannot settle faster than ~1/Δf = 1/(0.069 f): 370 ms at 39 Hz, 185 ms at 78 Hz [A §1]) and is absent for the physically normal end state of a howl (plateau at a limiter/compressor level, no growth, no clip [L §1.4]). This design replaces the sum with **tracked spectral lines** and a short **decision list of independent physical predicates**; each threshold is a physical quantity, each emitted `Detection.reasons` names the predicates that fired (for the notch report).

### 1.1 Per-frame front end (`feed`, detector.py:1072)
* Spectrum history: last 64 frames, and the **spectrum reference** `ref` = median of bands 25..85 (110 Hz–7 kHz, where the analyser settles within a frame) — the common-mode signal [A §5].
* `prominence[i]` = level − median of ±3 neighbours (unchanged; `prominences()` kept).
* **Narrowness** (`_narrowness`, :567): peak minus the *shallower* of its two flanks, a flank being the lower of the two bands just outside the line's footprint (a line between two centres reads (−3,−3) in a band pair [A §3], so the pair is the footprint). A lone sinusoid leaks only through the analyser skirts (≥12 dB down at ±2 even for 2nd-order skirts [A §3]); a formant hump, cymbal wash, PA ripple or applause has no dip on either side. `narrow_db` 8 (tracked from 4). Survives another line two bands away (X11 in a loud band), which "peak vs ±2" did not.
* **Centroid** (`_centroid`, :588): power centroid of peak±1 → sub-band frequency (±0.05 band); absorbs edge-straddling and vibrato band-hopping [A §4.4]; `Detection.freq_hz` is the *interpolated* frequency so the notch planner picks the right 1/3-octave band for a line between RTA centres [L §2.1/§4.1].
* Lines (local max, prominence ≥ `track_prominence_db` 6, narrowness ≥ 4) are matched to **tracks** by nearest centroid within ±1.35 band (a hand-held mic re-locking ≤160 c stays one track [L §2.3]); hysteresis ≤12 frames; a track that re-appears within 10 frames on its extrapolated ramp is resurrected (a crash hides a growing ring, S3/S14). A matched line >0.6 band from the track's home is a *different* line (melody step) and is born anew — unless the track is an already-reported ring (hop). Newborn tracks are **back-filled** from the spectrum history with the frames in which their band was already a local maximum (`_backfill`, :1263): a 150 dB/s howl in a loud mix shows 2–4 frames of growth and none may be wasted.
* Each track records how it was **born**: `est` (present at arm), `pop` (≥55 % of its rise above the pre-birth level arrived in one frame step: an instrument onset — or a >500 dB/s ring, the irreducible case [L §1.3, C §7.10]), `static`, `new`/`grow`.

### 1.3 Decision list (first match emits; :1425-1505)
1. **Already reported** (sticky): re-emit every `cooldown_s` while prominent, narrow, not decaying **and within 3 dB of the level it was reported at** (`sustained`); re-growth from lower down after a partial cut ⇒ `regrow`. Family appearing after the verdict is ignored (distortion products appear near clip [A §4.3]).
2. **CLIP**: ≥ −0.5 dBFS, narrow, plateau stationary/steady, 3 frames, ≥160 Hz.
3. **LOUD**: ≥ −10 dBFS, narrow, prominent, plateau stationary+steady 3 frames (150 ms), not decaying, ≥160 Hz, no family unless ≥ −6, no probe-linear veto, not sync-onset.
4. **GROW-fast/slow**: G ∧ narrow ∧ stationary ∧ prominence ≥10.5 ∧ ¬co-moving family — *any birth class* (catches rings seeded by a speech/choir partial X9/X21 and re-growth after a fader move X12) and *any rate*.
5. **EST**: born at arm ∧ narrow ∧ prominent ∧ stationary ∧ steady ∧ not decaying ∧ ¬family ∧ ≥160 Hz ∧ level ≥ −40 ∧ within 4 dB of its arm level ∧ 5 frames ∧ ¬probe-linear ∧ ¬musical. 250 ms after arm at any prominence ≥12 (M7's 15-s miss; X7 at 15–25 dB).
6. **PROBE** (steps noted): (2 hits ∨ 1 ≥4 dB) ∧ narrow ∧ prominence ≥9 ∧ stationary ∧ not decaying ∧ ¬family → cut *before* runaway [L §4.3].
7. Otherwise candidate only (reasons `popped-onset`, `family`, `moving`, `decaying` published). **A line that popped into existence and sits flat below −10 dBFS is never cut on passive evidence** (organ X1, flute X2, whistles X3/S8c/S20/X18, sine lead X4, 808 X5/S7, bells S24, hum X20): the irreducible window [C §7.10] resolved toward the human in `watch` and the probe in `ring_out`.
`confidence` = monotone function of margins, ≥ `confidence_threshold` iff emitted (dashboard contract); not the decision variable.

### 1.4 Modes
`DetectorConfig.mode` / `FeedbackDetector(cfg, band_hz, mode=)`: `watch` | `ringout`. Same decision list; `ringout` receives `note_gain_step(delta_db, ts)` (cfs.py:1196-1202 calls it after every master write; cfs.py:891 passes the session mode) enabling rule 6, the probe-linear veto and the step-locked 4 dB growth threshold. `note_cut()` accepted (reserved). Without steps `ringout` behaves as `watch`.
